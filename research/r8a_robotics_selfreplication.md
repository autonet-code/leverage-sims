# r8a: Robotics production, self-replication, industrial explosion (as of 2026-09-30)

## Current state (hard data)
- Industrial robot installs: 542k (2024, IFR WR2025), 603k (2025, +11%, IFR WR2026). China 354k (59%), +20%.
  10-yr growth 2014-2024 ~2.45x => CAGR ~9.4%, doubling ~7.7 yr. IFR forecast >700k/yr by 2028.
- Operational stock: 4.66M (2024, +9%), ~5M+ (2025). Stock doubling ~6-8 yr (Forethought: last doubling took 6 yr).
- Humanoids: ~13-14k shipped 2025 (Omdia/others), ~80-85% Chinese (AgiBot ~5.2k, Unitree ~5.5k). Roughly 3-4x YoY from a small base.
  Global humanoid stock < 100k. Humanoid units ~2% of industrial robot installs.
- 2026 targets: Unitree 20k, BYD 20k, Tesla stated 50-100k (actual mid-2026 est. ~1-1.2k, unconfirmed; Fremont line "starting soon" as of Q2 call).
  Goldman raised 2026 forecast to 75k, 2030 to 890k, 2035 to 6.5M (Sep 2026).
- Announced capacity: Tesla Fremont gen-1 line 1M/yr design, Giga Texas gen-2 10M/yr long-term; Figure BotQ 12k/yr; Agility RoboFab 10k+/yr; UBTech 5k/yr.
  Announced vs delivered track record is poor (Tesla 2025 target thousands, delivered hundreds at most).
- Cost: Unitree R1 $4.9-5.9k (limited, no working hands); G1 ~$16k; industrial-grade humanoids $50-150k+. Robot costs falling ~40%/yr (Forethought).
- Lights-out / robots-building-robots: Fanuc Oshino has run robot production unattended up to 30 days since ~2001 (~50 robots/shift); humans still do
  final assembly, maintenance, and upstream supply. Xiaomi Changping: 10M phones/yr, ~81% automated, humans for maintenance/supervision.
  No actor has closed the loop (mining, refining, chips, energy, logistics, construction). Share of robot value-added done without humans: unknown, likely <20%.
- Capability (Epoch, 2026): warehouse picking >99% in production; most manipulation 3-10x slower than humans; lab laundry 75-95%;
  furniture assembly, cooking early research. Transfer/generalization is the main bottleneck. Brooks: dexterity poor through 2036 (skeptic prior).
- Robot foundation models: Gemini Robotics (ER 2, Jul 2026), pi0.6 (RL from experience), Figure Helix, GR00T, Qwen-RobotManip. Rapid benchmark gains, limited real deployment.
- China 15th FYP (2026-30): embodied intelligence as future industry, 1T RMB fund; humanoid commercialization expected late in plan period.

## Physical constraints
- Mines: discovery-to-production ~15.7-16.5 yr (S&P, IEA); brownfield expansion 2-5 yr.
- Leading-edge fab: ~3.5 yr groundbreaking to HVM (TSMC AZ fab 1), ~2 yr target with compression.
- Energy: 2025 global renewables +692 GW (solar 511 GW), China ~315 GW AC solar. Energy buildout is fast relative to mining.

## Industrial explosion models
- Forethought (Davidson & Hadshar 2025): stages = AI-directed humans (~10x one-off productivity), autonomous robot factories, nanotech.
  Initial robot-stock doubling ~1 yr once AI directs production (payback 5 mo to 1-2 yr), later months/weeks; biological bound days-weeks.
  Industrial explosion starts after the intelligence explosion; slower due to physical feedback loops.
- Roodman 2020, Davidson 2021 explosive growth, Epoch GATE: explosive (>30%/yr GWP) growth plausible conditional on full automation incl. physical;
  these are conditional, not forecasts of timing.

## Verdict on user claim 3
Partly true. "Robots building robots" exists (Fanuc since 2001) but as automated segments within human-dependent supply chains, not closed self-replication.
"Exponential" growth: industrial robots ~9-11%/yr (exponential but slow, ~7 yr doubling); humanoids ~3-4x/yr from tiny base, announced capacity far above output.
Nothing today is a self-expanding industrial base; the coupling AI -> robot design/production is real and accelerating but unproven at scale.
v2 was wrong to have no AI->robotics coupling and no robots->robot-production feedback; user is wrong that the loop is already closing.
