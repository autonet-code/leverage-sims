## Software / cognitive
- Google: AI share of new code 25% (Oct 2024) -> ~50% (Oct 2025 / Feb 2026) -> 75% (Apr 2026), "AI-generated and approved by engineers" [V digitalapplied, secondtalent]. ~+33pp/yr.
- Anthropic: "pretty much 100%" of code written by Claude (Jan 2026, Fortune); Claude "leads" 26% of Anthropic AI R&D work by person-time, >90% of tasks at "collaborates" or higher (Sep 2026) [V]. OpenAI: 99.8% of worker output tokens via Codex (Jun 2026; tool usage, not autonomy) [V].
- Shopify >50%, Airbnb ~60% co-authored, Uber >10% fully autonomous agents (May 2026). Microsoft 20-30% (Apr 2025, no update) [V].
- Definition matters: "AI-written, human-reviewed" (50-100% at leaders) vs "autonomous agent, no human in loop" (~10% at Uber, low elsewhere).
- METR 50% time horizon: Opus 4.6 ~12 h (80%: 70 min); best shared model early 2026 ~16-20 h (80%: 3-4 h), at edge of benchmark range. Doubling ~4.3 mo (2023+), ~3-3.5 mo (2024-25) vs 7 mo long-run [V METR, LessWrong].
- Economy-wide: genAI in ~6.3% of US work hours (Q2 2026, St Louis Fed), ~28%/yr growth; firm production use ~18% flat H1 2026 (BTOS); API usage ~75% automation-mode [P r8e].

## Industrial robots / factories
- Installs 2025: 603k world (+11%), China 354k (59%, +20%); stock ~5M (+9%). China elec/electronics 96.4k (+16%), auto 78.9k (+38%), metal/machinery 78.7k (+44%); textiles -92%, food -37% (new segments volatile). IFR outlook China +5-10%/yr to 2029 [V IFR WR2026].
- Density 2024: Korea ~1,012, Singapore ~770, China ~470 (3rd, passed Germany/Japan), Germany ~430, Japan ~420, US ~300 per 10k mfg workers [K from IFR WR2025, medium].
- Dark factories: Xiaomi Changping 10M phones/yr, 11 fully automated lines; Fanuc robots-building-robots unattended up to 30 days since 2001; ASE 56 lights-out packaging/test plants [V wiki]. Humans remain for maintenance, changeover, upstream supply. Lights-out share of manufacturing value-added: no official stat; estimate <2% globally, 1-4% China [K, low].

## Humanoids
- 2025 ~13-14k shipped; 1H 2026 19.1k (+272% YoY), 97% Chinese, AgiBot 44%, Unitree 31% [V SAG, Pandaily]. Forbes: up to ~30k YTD by Aug 2026. TrendForce: China output +94% in 2026. Full-year 2026 ~40-60k [V/K]. Deployment mostly pilots, education, research; productive factory use small.

## Mobility / logistics
- Waymo: 500k paid rides/wk, 3,871 vehicles (Jun 2026), ~2x/yr rides, 3x miles Sep24-Sep25 [V wiki]. ~1-2% of US ride-hail. Baidu Apollo Go similar scale in China, expanding to Dubai 2026 [V/K].
- Driverless highway trucks: Kodiak (Dec 2024, private roads), Aurora a handful of trucks in Texas 2025-26 [V]. <0.1% of freight.
- Mining AHS: Cat + Komatsu ~1,500-2,000 autonomous haul trucks (2025); China ~1,500-3,000 driverless mining trucks, fastest growing (EACON, Waytous, state coal) [K, medium]. Global large haul fleet ~40-50k -> ~6-10% share, +20-30%/yr. Rio Tinto/BHP Pilbara iron ore is mostly autonomous haul + AutoHaul rail.
- Ports: ~70-100 automated/semi-automated container terminals of ~1,000+, ~5-10% of capacity; China leads (Qingdao, Tianjin, Shanghai Yangshan) [K, medium]. Growth slow, a few terminals/yr.
- Warehouses: Amazon ~1M robots (2025), approaching parity with human staff count [K, medium].

## Construction
- Construction robotics <1% of work (layout, rebar tying, bricklaying pilots, 3D printing). Slowest segment [K, low-medium].

## Military
- Ukraine: ~2M+ drones made 2024, ~4M+ 2025 target; drones cause ~60-80% of casualties on the front (RUSI and Ukrainian MoD estimates); AI terminal guidance on a small but rising share (few %); thousands of UGVs ordered 2025-26 [K, medium]. Russia comparable volumes. PLA: "intelligentized warfare" doctrine, drone swarm demos, no public count.
- Fully autonomous (no human in the targeting loop) lethal use: rare, but terminal autonomy spreading under jamming.

## Takeaways for model
1. Cognitive automation is not a 2030 milestone: AI-written code is already majority at leading firms (50-100%); what remains is autonomy length and reliability. Define milestones by METR horizon at 80% reliability (now ~3-4 h) and by "share of work done without human review".
2. Physical automation is real but thin: dark factories exist as islands; robot stock grows ~9-11%/yr (China ~20%), humanoids ~3x/yr from a tiny base, mining AHS ~20-30%/yr, robotaxis ~2x/yr. Nothing physical yet grows at AI's ~10x/yr.
3. The gap between announced/pilot and productive deployment is large for humanoids; for mining/robotaxi/warehouse it is real productive work.
