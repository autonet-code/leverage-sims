## Assessment of user claim 4 (signals of intent)
- Holds up: strong, documented signals of power accumulation and control-seeking (compute race, state-firm fusion, regulatory capture spending, military autonomy, surveillance, Musk's robot-army control remark), and of indifference/hand-waving on labor (UBI -> UBC, "Jevons" reframing, lobbying against state rules).
- Also holds: restraint gets punished (Anthropic episode); human-in-loop norms are being rewritten.
- Does not hold: no documented or credible evidence of intent to depopulate or cull. Bunkers are consistent with fear of war/unrest (PREP), not planning harm. Public stated aims (abundance, "eliminate poverty", UBC) are self-serving but point to token provision, not elimination.
- Counter-signals: Senate 99-1 against preemption; courts overturned Anthropic designation; Stargate expansion collapsed on financing; Amodei softening. Checks still function in 2026.
- Model implication: signals should raise P(single actor pursues full-loop control / power concentration) and lower the "public stays useful so rulers restrain" brake, but should not raise P(deliberate depopulation intent) much. Depopulation in the model should arise (if at all) from indifference + no-need-for-labor + neglect/attrition, not from signalled intent.
