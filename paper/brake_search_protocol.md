# Brake search protocol (fixed 2026-10-02, before any search)

Purpose: search for restraining mechanisms ("brakes") on rulers that the model may be missing, with the same rigor that went into removing brakes that contradicted observed 2026 conditions.

## Inclusion rules (all three required)
1. **Documented mechanism.** A causal account, in published research, of how the brake restrains people who hold power.
2. **Historical restraint.** At least two historical cases where the brake restrained rulers who had the capacity to carry out mass repression or killing and did not, or stopped. The case evidence must attribute the restraint to this mechanism, not just record that restraint happened.
3. **Model-expressible.** It can be stated as a parameter or rule in the existing model, and it is not already carried by an existing parameter. Existing parameters include moral cost, stigma, altruism toward the public, elite refusal, the inner-circle veto, outside pressure, revolt threat, discounting and nuclear deterrence.

## Survival under the scenario's conditions
A brake that works only through human labor, human enforcers or public revolt leverage is already removed by assumptions A3 to A6. It is recorded, but it is only included if its mechanism survives those conditions.

## Strengths
- Ranges come from the historical cases.
- Where cases cannot bound the size, the range comes from a Jev elicitation: pre-specified wording, three paraphrases, and the median and spread reported.
- Weak evidence means a wide range, never a zero.

## Verification
Each candidate is checked by two independent adversarial reviewers:
- one tests the mechanism and the cases;
- one tests whether the brake is already in the model and whether it survives A3 to A6.

A candidate is included only if neither reviewer refutes it.

## Reporting
The paper lists every candidate considered, whether it passed, why any failed, and how much each included brake moves the result, both alone and together.

## Roles
Nobody chooses brakes or strengths after this protocol is fixed.

## Addendum (2026-10-02, before the follow-up search)
A review of the included "self-risk" brake found that its model fit is one-sided. It counts a coalition member's risk from voting FOR depopulation, through later paranoia and purges, but not the risk from voting AGAINST, where the dissenter is targeted. The opposite mechanism, "dissent risk", is therefore searched under the same rules (mechanism, two or more cases, model fit, adversarial verification, evidence-based strength). The self-risk brake is re-audited on two points: whether a held depopulation ideology should weaken it, and whether the Jev elicitation that sized it was balanced. The leader-mortality hazard is corrected to the form its own basis describes (set at closure, falling with medicine, not compounding through successors). The inclusion rules are unchanged.
The follow-up search also covers (a) fear of posthumous judgment (divine, historical or legacy) as a possible brake that weakens as life extension makes death remote, and (b) a longer expected life lengthening the horizon over which surviving witnesses are a threat (a possible amplifier of A11). Both are held to the same inclusion rules.
